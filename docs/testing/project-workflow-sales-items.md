# Project workflow sales-item scope

Catalogue: IT-WORKFLOW-010. User decision: the project workflow must contain items from sales invoices only, not purchase-only items; on shipments, keep normal packing, quantity, QC and document safeguards for purchase-only items without requiring nonexistent sales-artwork/PPS workflow approvals.

## Acceptance (2026-11-01)

- Project-wide rules remain project-wide. Product workflow rules use only active, non-cancelled project products represented on a non-cancelled sales proforma, tax invoice or cash invoice for the same project. Sales and purchase documents that refer to the same project product create one lane; purchase-only or cross-project items cannot create or trigger product workflow rules. Draft sales documents remain included. Converted sales documents do not duplicate lanes.
- Existing purchase-only workflow rows, evaluations, approvals, overrides and source records are preserved as history, but are excluded from active calculations, checks, closure prerequisites, actions, product lists, approval choices and workflow counts. A direct stale URL cannot create a new bypass/approval on an excluded item. Financial snapshots still include actual outstanding purchase obligations.
- For old sales lines without a `project_product_id`, an unambiguous same-project product ID or name match keeps the real sales item in scope without changing its source line; ambiguous or unmatched legacy lines are surfaced for review rather than matched to an arbitrary product. Saving an invoice must not turn an ambiguous name/catalogue match or unrelated-project explicit link into an arbitrary association. An intentional project reassignment can rematerialize the old project's line on the new project only when the new match is unambiguous.
- Removing or cancelling the final eligible sales source removes its product checks from active scope; another remaining sales document keeps them active. Reinstating a previously excluded lane must not silently reuse an obsolete bypass as approval for new work. No unrelated invoice, ledger, access-control or shipment validation may be weakened.
- Workflow history groups repeated product checks by rule and labels each product, with separate per-product controls and truthful rule/check counts. Concurrent syncs are serialized and must not create duplicate records for the same product and rule, including under MySQL repeatable-read snapshots; pre-existing conflicting duplicates remain visible as a warning and are not deleted.
- Shipment lines that are not sales workflow items retain existing packing, quantity, QC and document checks; sales items continue to require their own artwork and PPS decisions. Project-level payment and dispatch rules remain in force.
- When invoice lines materialise project products, take the project lock before changing a product and refreshing its workflow. This avoids an inverted purchase-product/workflow lock order during the tested concurrent save and synchronization; it is not a guarantee against every possible database deadlock.
